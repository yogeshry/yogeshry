# Yogesh Rai

Ph.D. student in Computer Science (HCI and visual analytics) at the University of Alabama in Huntsville, advised by Dr. Haeyong Chung. Research funded by NASA IMPACT (Marshall Space Flight Center).

I build visual analytics systems for two problems: how people inspect and trust what language models do, and how people interact with data across phones, tablets, desktops, and AR headsets.

## Research

- **Visual analytics for LLM interpretability (current).** Mechanistic explanation and feature attribution that show how a language model makes design decisions, such as mark and encoding choices, when it generates Vega-Lite and Altair specifications.
- **SIGMA: a spatial interaction grammar for multi-display visual analytics.** A declarative JSON grammar of display entities, spatial predicates composed from 11 primitive relations, and mapping rules; evaluated against 96 interaction techniques coded from 34 papers; runtime in Unity on HoloLens 2 streaming to D3 and Vega-Lite clients. *IEEE VIS 2026*, full paper (first author).
- **Walking Through Time: hybrid tablet and AR timeline authoring.** A HoloLens 2 application for drawing, branching, and annotating non-linear timelines in space, synchronized with a tablet. *IEEE VIS 2025*, short paper (second author).
